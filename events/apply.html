Security & Storage

  • ✓ Private Storage: Submissions and pitch decks are stored in isolated, access-controlled buckets.
  • ✓ Supported Files: PDF presentations, Word documents (.docx), and high-res images (.png, .jpg).
  • ✓ Size Limit: Max 10MB per uploaded file. Up to 5 files per application.
  • ✓ Row Level Security: Submissions are readable only by authorized club mentors and administrators.

Review Process

  1. 1. Submission Received

    You'll receive an instant confirmation and reference ID.

  2. 2. Mentor Evaluation

    The tech and venture panel reviews problem statement & deck.

  3. 3. Shortlist Drop

    Shortlisted teams are contacted via WhatsApp and email for pitch rounds.

💡 Pitch Tip

Focus on the problem you are solving, your target market, and why your team has the unfair advantage to build it. A 5-slide deck is better than a 30-page essay.
